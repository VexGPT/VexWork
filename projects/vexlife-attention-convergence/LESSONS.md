# VexLife Attention Convergence — lessons

[VXG RealForever]

## Many connections do not imply many simultaneous thoughts

A durable connection can remain current and reachable without occupying the present context.

~~~text
CONNECTED
!= PRESENTLY ATTENDED
!= PROMPT-RESIDENT
~~~

## VexCgmLabs is reusable pressure, not a one-off alert feature

The transferable lesson is source/Signal/Observation/semantic ownership.

CGM-specific meaning stays CGM-owned.

## Activity relevance and source-required attention are different

~~~text
ACTIVITY-SCOPED PULL
  current human activity makes one connected source useful now

SOURCE-REQUIRED PUSH
  source-owned current meaning requires awareness regardless of topic
~~~

Do not collapse them into one relevance score.

## Urgency belongs to the source relationship

Attention Convergence must not invent why a Health, storage, security or device condition matters.

It only carries an already-owned claim on present awareness.

## Attention may move while work remains

~~~text
DISPLACED WORK
!= LOST WORK
~~~

An interruptible Vex should also be resumable.

## Acknowledgement is not resolution

The human seeing/acknowledging something and the source condition becoming false/currently resolved are separate truths.

## Quiet cost matters

A future Vex may have dozens or hundreds of connections.

The design fails if every quiet connection expands the prompt or requires expensive scanning every turn.

## Self-Inspection explains; it does not arbitrate

The body should be able to answer why attention shifted, what source owns the claim, what was preserved and what remains outside present residency.

## Human and Vex should see the same body truth

Settings/Vex Context and Self-Inspection may use different presentations, but should descend to the same current owners.

## Do not solve scale by erasing source identity

If several sources are simultaneously relevant/urgent, keep them separate.

~~~text
CONVERGENCE
!= SEMANTIC COLLAPSE
~~~
