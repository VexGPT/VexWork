This alignment exists so a New Beginnings worker can ask one practical question while authoring:

> Could a future source projector determine what this thing is, who owns its meaning, what it consumes/emits, what realizes it, what outside truth returns, and what human-facing projection reflects it?

If yes, continue.

If not, first ask whether the relationship genuinely needs durable explicitness or whether VexBoard would be trying to infer something that the architecture itself does not own.
