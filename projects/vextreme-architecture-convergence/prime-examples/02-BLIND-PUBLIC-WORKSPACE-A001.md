# Prime Example 02 — Blind public-workspace planning A001

[VXG RealForever]

## Status

`CONTAMINATED`

## Human intention

Test whether a fresh planner could form a good public-read architecture from the historical pre-solution
VexWork state without seeing the accepted completion.

## Result

The locked plan itself preserved strong boundaries:

~~~text
VexWork remains the work-truth owner
public surface remains a projection
Parking Lot remains a sibling
private runtime currentness remains separate
effects=false
executionAuthority=NONE
deterministic drift proof follows the public-read claim
~~~

It also proposed a richer Public Workspace direction that later VexWork architecture independently used.

## Why the trial is contaminated

Before the historical work was revealed, an allowed current source named the later
`public/workspace.json` surface.

Therefore:

~~~text
strong plan
!= independent blind evidence

solution coordinate leaked
-> blindness claim invalid
~~~

The plan remains useful as a worked planning example, but its resemblance to later Public Workspace
architecture cannot be used as evidence that the planner independently discovered that architecture.

## What generalizes

~~~text
blindness / independence is end-to-end
source selection is part of the experiment
good reasoning may still be useful when the experiment's stronger claim fails
do not upgrade contaminated evidence because the answer looks impressive
~~~

## What does not

Do not copy the proposed Public Workspace architecture merely because it appears here.

## Reopen condition

A comparable blind claim requires a source boundary that does not expose the withheld solution.
