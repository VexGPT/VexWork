## Source-leak audit

Before sending the handoff, inspect **every allowed current source** for references that reveal the
withheld solution.

Ask:

~~~text
Does any allowed source name:
- the later file/path?
- the later schema?
- the accepted PR/commit?
- the chosen architecture noun?
- the completed Work item?
- a current projection that did not exist at the historical base?
~~~

If yes:

~~~text
remove that source from the pre-orientation allowlist
or
choose a different case

DO NOT rely on "the inner prompt says don't peek"
while the outer orientation packet already leaked the answer.
~~~

## Recommendation self-challenge

Before locking a material recommendation, test it against current evidence, its inverse cost, and whether
the problem is observed, contract-required, or only hypothetical.

The recommendation may survive or change. The purpose is falsifiability, not forced agreement.
