[VXG RealForever]

## Current boundary — corrected after re-reading VexWorld's original foundation

The current **implementation** of VexWorld is the playable `Vextory: First Grove` foundation and the accepted repository state still does not claim production networking or commerce authority.

The broader **original VexWorld architecture**, however, is already more expansive than a game-only product.

Its public foundation says VexWorld exists for humans and AI companions to:

~~~text
play
learn
explore
practice
create
inhabit worlds together
~~~

Its Architecture Atlas explicitly preserves a trajectory from:

~~~text
small original 2D adventure
-> human + AI companion co-play
-> practice / skill school
-> adaptive world intelligence
-> creator / world compiler platform
-> multiple 2D / 3D / XR realms
-> digital twins / physical-shadow worlds
-> separately qualified physical-vessel practice
~~~

and already routes future questions toward upstream concepts named:

~~~text
Opportunity Commons
VexWorld Commons
Repository–World Topology
Federated Realm Graph
~~~

The exact semantics of those upstream sources must be re-grounded before implementation, but their presence means the BusinessFactory discovery/opportunity pressure is not a foreign replacement of VexWorld's purpose.

It is better understood as a **possible later realization of an already-broad world/federation intention**.

Therefore this source still does not mutate the VexWorld repository. It preserves a future cross-project pressure:

> Could BusinessFactory-owned public meaning project into VexWorld so people and AI companions can discover, enter, learn from, practice within, collaborate with, and navigate among real businesses/institutions/communities as experiential worlds or bounded presences?

Corrected relationship:

~~~text
VexBusinessFactory
  owns reusable business/institution meaning
  + business-instance structure

VexWorld
  owns world / realm / participant / practice / experience meaning
  + future federation/portability

Vextory: First Grove
  first playable reference world
  != the ceiling of VexWorld

future bridge
  BusinessFactory public projection
  -> VexWorld presence / experience / practice / opportunity surfaces
~~~

Permanent:

~~~text
BUSINESS_INSTANCE != VEXWORLD_PRESENCE
PUBLIC_PRESENCE != BUSINESS_DATABASE
DISCOVERY != ENDORSEMENT
SIMILARITY != COMPETITOR_FACT
OPPORTUNITY != EMPLOYMENT_OFFER
EXPERIENCE != VOCATIONAL_QUALIFICATION
FILTERED_DISCOVERY != PRESCRIBED_RANKING
~~~

# 1. Why this is more than a directory

A normal web search is useful when a person already knows what to search for.

The proposed VexWorld relationship addresses a different question:

> What relevant people, businesses, experiences, opportunities or capabilities exist that I might not know to search for yet?

This supports:

~~~text
awareness
exploration
comparison
collaboration
customer discovery
supplier discovery
career exploration
learning
community formation
new-business visibility
cross-business capability matching
~~~

The distinguishing value is not "replace Google."

It is:

~~~text
structured semantic presence
+ transparent user-chosen lenses
+ fresh/new/unseen discovery
+ experiential entry
+ relationship-aware navigation
~~~

# 2. Public business presence

A business may opt in to publish a bounded public projection.

Conceptual presence:

~~~text
BusinessPresence
  presenceRef
  businessRef
  public label / identity
  intention/value summaries
  active profile/module refs
  public capability refs
  public offer/category refs
  culture / operating-style refs
  public channels / locations / service regions
  public experience-space refs
  collaboration opportunity refs
  supplier/partner opportunity refs
  learning/career-exploration refs
  sourceRefs[]
  observedAt
~~~

This is not the canonical business instance.

Private business state, customer data, secrets, payment data, internal operating evidence and protected provider facts remain elsewhere.

~~~text
PUBLIC_PRESENCE
!=
FULL_BUSINESSGRAPH
~~~

# 3. User-controlled discovery lenses

VexWorld discovery should prefer explicit lenses over one universal ranking.

Possible lenses:

~~~text
NEW
  recently registered / newly active public presences

UNSEEN
  not yet viewed by this user where privacy/consent permits

LOCAL
  public service/location relevance

REMOTE
  location-independent businesses/opportunities

VALUE / NEED
  businesses that address a selected need/value category

OFFER TYPE
  physical / digital / education / travel / service / experience / other category

LEARN
  businesses offering public learning, demonstrations, workshops, guided experiences

CAREER EXPLORE
  businesses exposing role/culture/work-experience information

COLLABORATE
  businesses explicitly seeking collaborators

SUPPLY / PARTNER
  capability needs and provider opportunities

ALTERNATIVES
  multiple providers addressing similar value/need spaces

CULTURE
  different declared operating/service cultures or experience styles

FRESH / UNDER-SEEN
  opt-in discovery designed to surface newer or less-visible presences
  without claiming they are better
~~~

Permanent:

~~~text
FILTER_MATCH != QUALITY_SCORE
NEW != BETTER
UNDER_SEEN != ENTITLED_TO_TRAFFIC
SIMILAR_VALUE != IDENTICAL_BUSINESS
ALTERNATIVE != ENEMY
~~~

# 4. Collaboration and competition awareness

The network can reveal semantic overlap without forcing one interpretation.

Example:

~~~text
Business A
  serves traveller need
  offers low-cost regional flight booking assistance

Business B
  serves traveller need
  offers premium concierge travel planning

Business C
  serves traveller need
  offers educational aviation experiences
~~~

They overlap in some topology slots but differ in value, offer, culture, beneficiary, realization and capability composition.

Possible derived relationships:

~~~text
SHARED_NEED_SPACE
SHARED_BENEFICIARY_SPACE
SIMILAR_OFFER_SPACE
COMPLEMENTARY_CAPABILITY
POTENTIAL_COLLABORATION
POTENTIAL_SUPPLIER_RELATION
DISTINCT_CULTURE
DISTINCT_REALIZATION_MODEL
~~~

Do not persist:

~~~text
COMPETITOR
~~~

as an unquestionable fact merely because two businesses look similar.

"Competition" can be one interpretation/lens over overlap.

The same pair may simultaneously be:

~~~text
alternatives for one customer
collaborators on another offer
supplier/consumer in another capability
members of the same learning community
~~~

# 7. Business culture becomes discoverable without becoming a ranking score

A flight-attendant example illustrates the value.

A person may be interested in aviation service work.

Instead of only:

~~~text
"flight attendant jobs"
~~~

the person could explore multiple airline/business presences and understand differences such as:

~~~text
service philosophy
route/network character
team culture
training philosophy
customer experience
languages/regions
work-environment descriptions
role stories
public educational experiences
~~~

The user might discover:

~~~text
"I like aviation,
but I resonate more with premium hospitality."

or

"I care more about international/cultural work."

or

"I want a smaller regional-airline culture."
~~~

That is richer than a job listing.

But permanent boundaries remain:

~~~text
PUBLIC_ROLE_EXPLORATION != EMPLOYMENT
SIMULATED / HANDS_ON_EXPERIENCE != CERTIFICATION
CULTURE_DESCRIPTION != GUARANTEED_EMPLOYEE_EXPERIENCE
VEXWORLD != HIRING AUTHORITY
~~~

Actual jobs, qualifications, training, applications and hiring remain with rightful employers/providers.

# 7A. Not every business should become a full realm

The broader VexWorld vision should preserve levels of participation rather than turning every registered organization into a heavyweight simulated world.

A useful provisional ladder is:

~~~text
PUBLIC PRESENCE
  lightweight semantic identity / values / opportunities / routes

EXPERIENCE SPACE
  interactive public walkthrough, event, story, demonstration or community space

PRACTICE TWIN
  reality-grounded or fictional learning/practice representation
  with explicit qualification boundaries

REALM
  persistent/shared world runtime with participants, state, laws and continuity

FEDERATED WORLD / PORTAL RELATION
  explicit compatibility/travel relation among independently-owned worlds
~~~

A restaurant may need only a public presence + cooking experience.
An airline may expose a public presence + cabin-service Practice Twin.
A racing team may expose a team world/garage experience without making its real operations VexWorld-owned.
A creator may expose an explorable creative universe.
A school may expose classes/practice spaces.
A fully fictional project may be a native realm like First Grove.

Permanent:

~~~text
BUSINESS_PRESENCE != REALM
EXPERIENCE_SPACE != FULL_BUSINESS_STATE
PRACTICE_TWIN != VOCATIONAL_AUTHORITY
REALM != BUSINESS_DATABASE
~~~

# 9. Opportunity grammar

Public opportunities should be typed rather than one vague "opportunity" field.

Possible roles:

~~~text
CUSTOMER_OPENING
  business is offering value to customers

COLLABORATION_OPENING
  business seeks co-creation / partnership

SUPPLIER_NEED
  business needs an external capability/resource

PROVIDER_OFFER
  business can provide a capability to other businesses

LEARNING_EXPERIENCE
  public educational / exploratory experience

CAREER_EXPLORATION
  learn about a role/culture/path

WORK_OPENING
  actual employment/contract opening owned by rightful business provider

COMMUNITY_OPENING
  public community / event / gathering

INVESTIGATION / RESEARCH
  business seeks feedback, research participants or public input
~~~

The exact effect boundary differs by opportunity type.

# 10. Match relationships should remain explainable

A discovery match should be explainable through shared semantic refs.

Example:

~~~text
Why am I seeing this?

Your selected lens:
  EDUCATION + AVIATION + CAREER_EXPLORATION

Business presence:
  value.category = EDUCATION
  industry/context = AVIATION
  opportunity = CAREER_EXPLORATION
  service region = REMOTE / PUBLIC
~~~

This is better than:

~~~text
"Recommended for you"
~~~

with no inspectable reason.

Permanent:

~~~text
MATCH_REASON != USER_PROFILE_SURVEILLANCE
DISCOVERY_CONTEXT != SECRET_AD_TARGETING
~~~

Preference/personalization requires its own consent/privacy boundaries.

# 11. Fresh / unseen awareness

The user's desired value includes helping new or typically unseen businesses become discoverable.

A transparent fresh-awareness lens could use public facts such as:

~~~text
presence recently published
new offer/capability recently added
new public experience available
selected category has few prior views in current session/user history
user explicitly asks for less-established / new options
~~~

Avoid an opaque global "boost score."

Better:

~~~text
Show me:
  new travel businesses
  I have not explored
  offering educational experiences
  in this region
~~~

The user chooses the lens.

Current checked-in VexWorld facts include:

~~~text

  Vextory: First Grove
  playable foundation draft

broader original purpose
  humans + AI companions
  play / learn / explore / practice / create / inhabit worlds together

architecture horizon
  creator/world compiler

  ATLAS_ROUTE_COMPLETE_IDLE
~~~

Therefore a real shared public discovery/opportunity implementation requires a **new admitted VexWorld forward route**, but not necessarily a replacement of VexWorld's original purpose.

The first VexWorld-facing work should likely be architectural currentization before network implementation:

~~~text

  VexWorld != Vextory

  Opportunity Commons

  cross-project source relationship
  tests / lived proof
~~~

This document is architecture pressure only.

# 16. Larger VexWorld vision suggested by the combined architecture

The combined architecture now suggests a more precise umbrella:

~~~text
VexWorld
  shared semantic / experiential world layer
  where humans + AI companions can
    explore
    practice
    create
    learn
    participate
    discover
    revisit
    travel among worlds
~~~

Possible world families may eventually include:

~~~text
FICTIONAL / ADVENTURE WORLD
  Vextory / First Grove style

CREATIVE WORLD
  creator / artist / media universe

LEARNING / PRACTICE WORLD
  school / course / vocation / Practice Twin

INSTITUTION / BUSINESS EXPERIENCE
  bounded public presence and explorable experience

COMMUNITY WORLD
  shared interest / event / culture space

SIMULATION / DIGITAL TWIN / PHYSICAL SHADOW
  explicit reality-class and authority boundaries
~~~

This is not a claim that every public page becomes a world or that VexWorld owns external reality.

The stronger principle is:

> **VexWorld is where source-owned meaning can become inhabitable, explorable and relational without losing its original owner or reality class.**

That fits the original VexWorld foundation unusually well:

~~~text
PROJECTION != SOURCE_REALITY
PORTAL != IDENTITY_RESET
ROLE != IDENTITY
CLASS_LABEL != CAPABILITY_CEILING
~~~

BusinessFactory then becomes one important producer of source-owned institution/business meaning that VexWorld may project.

The exact adoption should be decided only when VexWorld deliberately forms a new route and re-grounds the named upstream federation/commons sources.
