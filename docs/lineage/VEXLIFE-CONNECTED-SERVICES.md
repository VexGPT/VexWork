## Lessons not to relearn

1. Visible progress matters; healthy-but-silent waits are not acceptable lived proof.
2. Prove the claimed effect, not a proxy predicate.
3. Attempted != active != observed != completed.
4. Protected human action must be followed by machine observation.
5. Recover exact owned state before replay; do not broad-reset.
6. Provider semantics matter: Serve, Funnel, and foreground ownership are distinct.
7. Filesystem canonicality is an invariant; repair the harness rather than weakening the owner.
8. Identity, reachability, session, membership, and authority stay separate.
9. Proof fixture != product architecture.
10. Receipt/failure label != semantic interpretation; inspect underlying evidence.
11. Before replay, retain the diagnostic that separates the next hypotheses.
12. Rebind current source after unrelated movement; do not blindly replay or blindly assume harmlessness.
13. Single-use attempt identity protects evidence.
14. Cleanup truth is part of the result.
15. The human should not become the harness operator.
16. Extract reusable architecture only after real consumers earn it.
17. Completion beats ceremony.
